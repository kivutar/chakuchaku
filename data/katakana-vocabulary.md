# Katakana vocabulary curriculum

The Katakana curriculum is a deliberately small beginner vocabulary layer, not
a claim about an official current JLPT N5 list. It uses the former JLPT Level 4
vocabulary specification as a stable exam-oriented baseline and adds practical
words that make the writing system teachable through complete words.

The app dictionary contains 159 all-Katakana entries representing 158 unique
written words. The N5 layer still contains the original 120 entries and 119
unique words; enabling N4 adds 39 words. `キロ` occurs twice because the legacy
vocabulary distinguishes kilograms from kilometers. The pool consists of:

- 63 core entries: the existing open N5 data plus `グラス`, `コピー`, and
  `スリッパ`, which restore the omissions found during comparison with the
  former Level 4 list.
- 20 previously curated supplemental entries, including `ラーメン`, `アニメ`,
  `コンビニ`, `スマホ`, and lesson vocabulary.
- 37 new Katakana-curriculum entries selected for beginner usefulness and
  writing-system coverage.
- 39 N4 entries from the pinned Open Anki snapshot and the curated `ボール`
  gap, including `アルバイト`, `コンサート`, `コンピュータ`, `スーツケース`,
  `サンドイッチ`, and `レジ`.

`ラジオカセ` was normalized to the usual `ラジカセ`; the longer form remains
indexed with its own reading so existing authored text can still resolve it.

## Selection rules

Words are preferred when they are concrete, useful in beginner conversation,
and supported by the former Level 4 inventory or an A1 beginner curriculum.
The supplemental layer also fills otherwise missing basic kana with natural
words. Proper nouns, brands, obscure transliterations, and words chosen solely
to complete every possible contracted-sound chart are excluded.

The new legacy-baseline entries are:

- `グラス`, `コピー`, `スリッパ`

The A1 and everyday beginner additions are:

- Food: `オレンジジュース`, `コーラ`, `サラダ`, `ジュース`, `チーズ`,
  `トマト`, `バーベキュー`, `ミルク`, `ヨーグルト`, `ウインナー`,
  `シュウマイ`, `キャベツ`, `チャーハン`
- Home and objects: `エアコン`, `キッチン`, `シャンプー`, `タオル`,
  `ドライヤー`, `プレゼント`, `ベランダ`, `マンション`, `ヘルメット`,
  `リュック`
- Activities, sports, and transportation: `サッカー`, `ジョギング`,
  `ショッピング`, `スーパー`, `テニス`, `バイク`, `カヌー`, `ゴルフ`,
  `フェスティバル`, `モノレール`
- Clothing and travel: `ジャケット`, `ファッション`, `チケット`,
  `ユニフォーム`

## Coverage

Small vowels and small `ャュョ` are joined to the preceding full-size kana as
one learning item. Small `ッ` and the long-vowel mark `ー` remain independent
items because the learner must recognize and type them explicitly.

- Basic Katakana: 45 of 46. Only `ヲ` is absent; forcing it into a loanword
  exercise would produce an unnatural beginner word.
- Voiced and semi-voiced Katakana: 22 of 25. `ゾ`, `ヂ`, and `ヅ` are omitted
  rather than supported with unusually advanced or artificial examples.
- Standard contracted sounds represented naturally: `キャ`, `キュ`, `シャ`,
  `シュ`, `ショ`, `チャ`, `チョ`, `ニュ`, `リュ`, `ジャ`, `ジュ`, `ジョ`.
- Common foreign-sound combinations represented: `ファ`, `フィ`, `フェ`,
  `フォ`, and `ティ`.

The inventory is large enough to launch the Katakana exercise without claiming
that every rare spelling combination belongs at N5. Later vocabulary growth can
add a second or third example for low-frequency items without changing the
initial curriculum boundary.
